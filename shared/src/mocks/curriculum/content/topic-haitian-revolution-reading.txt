[en] Saint-Domingue and revolution
Before independence, Haiti was the French colony of Saint-Domingue. An uprising by enslaved people began in northern Saint-Domingue in 1791. The Haitian Revolution ran from 1791 to 1804 and led to an independent Haiti. Enslaved people acted to challenge their conditions; this was not simply a copy of events in France.

[es] Saint-Domingue y revolución
Antes de la independencia, Haití era la colonia francesa de Saint-Domingue. En 1791 comenzó una rebelión de personas esclavizadas en el norte de la colonia. La Revolución haitiana se desarrolló entre 1791 y 1804 y dio lugar a un Haití independiente.
