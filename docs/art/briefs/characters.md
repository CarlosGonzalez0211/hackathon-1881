## Form language

- Proportions: adults 4.5 heads, children and the student 3.5 heads. Shoulders narrower than the head is wide; forearms slightly longer than upper arms; feet small.
- Head: one smooth mass from an SDF union (sphere plus a lower ellipsoid), small chin, cheek volumes. Eyes: large almonds, vertex-coloured on a slight dome, two highlights (one large, one small), soft brow sweeps, tiny mouth. Faces use `athena_style = 3` on eyes so the runtime keeps them unlit, `1` on skin.
- Hair: four to eight tapered swept locks over a smooth cap; a fringe lock crosses the forehead; buns, braids and ponytails are sweeps too. `athena_style = 2`.
- Body: a soft torso mass (not a lathe cylinder), hips and thighs as merged ellipsoids split at the joints, mitten hands with a thumb, boots with a rolled cuff. Garments are sculpted shells: poncho and capes with a wavy hem (profile lathe with a 6-fold scallop), coats with lapels as sweeps, aprons, skirts with a slight flare.
- Costume rule: each person wears a cape, hood or shawl in their signature colour (the runtime attaches a flowing cape to the chest node, so the sculpted garment should be a short mantle that the runtime cape extends), one signature prop, one gold detail.
- Student: hooded traveller; ochre poncho with a wavy hem and a chevron border via `athena_style = 7` and `athena_weave` uv; satchel; lantern staff in the left hand (`lamp_light` emissive); field journal on the right hip (`book_pages`); backpack `canvas_pack`.
- Lumen: one smooth body mass, swept tail, large calm eyes, glowing collar charm (`charm_glow`). Clips `idle`, `talk`, `expression_blink`.
- Owl, hare, visitor: same softness; keep silhouettes.

## Rig and clip contract (automated tests read this)

Node tree for people: `body > hip > {leg_l > knee_l, leg_r > knee_r, torso > {arm_l > elbow_l > hand_l, arm_r > elbow_r > hand_r, head > head_geo, face_expression}}` with `chest` under `torso` for the cape anchor and `shirt` or `top_detail` as the garment node the cape measures. Rigid meshes are split at joints (thigh under `leg_*`, calf and boot under `knee_*`, upper arm under `arm_*`, forearm and hand under `elbow_*`). Named meshes the rig test expects on the built student: `hair_cap` under `hair`, boot `upper` under `knee_l` and `upper_1` under `knee_r`, `traveller_hood` under `head`, `canvas_pack` under `torso`, `lamp_light` under `elbow_l`, `book_pages` under `elbow_r`. `userData.part` on every mesh (`base | hair | top | bottom | shoes | acc`). Clips: `idle, walk, run, sprint, air, afk1_lookaround, afk2_stretch, afk3_sit, talk, emote_wave, expression_blink` for people; `idle, talk, expression_blink` for Lumen; `idle, talk` plus `flap` or `flutter` where the current models have them. Blink: `face_expression.scale.y` below 0.2 at 3.82 s of `sprint` and above 0.99 at 4.12 s. Knees and elbows animate in every locomotion clip. Attributes named `athena_style` and `athena_weave` in the generator. Garment material names avoid `skin|hair|eye|blush|lips|sole|shoe|paper|ink`; tops start with `top` so the cape picks their colour. No emissive or transparent materials on characters except `lamp_light`, `charm_glow` and the visitor's antenna tips. Export `RIG_CONTRACT` from `athena-assets/code/rig/skeleton.mjs` and rewrite `tests/village-rig.test.ts` against it.

## Budgets

Avatar base at most 5k triangles, each part 1.5k, each NPC at most 7k total and 60 KB after meshopt; all NPCs together at most 1.2 MB first load. Each character merges to at most two skinned draws.

## Acceptance checklist

1. 64 px silhouette test: the student, Lumen and at least ten NPCs are distinguishable by hood, hat, hair or prop.
2. Asset sheet at yaws 0/90/180/270 plus the `walk` pose: no capsule limbs, no sphere heads, visible mitten thumbs, swept hair, wavy hems.
3. `avatar-portrait` and `npc-closeup` world shots: eyes read in shadow, cape attaches at the shoulders, no clipping between garment and body in `idle`, `walk`, `sprint`, `afk3_sit`.
4. Rig and kit tests green; interaction and navigation unchanged.
5. Budgets green; first-load total reported.
